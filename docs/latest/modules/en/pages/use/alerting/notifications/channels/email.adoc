= E-mail Notifications
:revdate: 2026-09-28
:page-revdate: {revdate}

{stackstate-product-name} can send real-time notification e-mail when state changes occur in the monitor. For example, when a monitor transitions to `CRITICAL`, `DEVIATION`, or back to `OK`.

This guide explains how to set up e-mail notification channels.

== Prerequisites
* Configure {stackstate-product-name} with the xref:setup/configure-stackstate/email-notifications.adoc#_configure_suse_observability_with_the_smtp_configuration[SMTP configuration].

== Configure the e-mail channel

image::k8s/email-notification.png[]

. Open the notifications page via the link in the bottom half of hamburger menu in the SUSE Observability UI. 

. Click the **Add new notification** button.

. In the **Add new notification** screen, select **Email** in the Channels section.

. Fill in the required channel parameters and click **ADD CHANNEL**.
.. **To**: Comma-separated list of target e-mail addresses to be notified (for example, `ops@example.com, dev-team@example.com`).
.. **Cc**: Comma-separated list of target e-mail addresses to be informed (for example, `ops@example.com, dev-team@example.com`).
.. **Subject prefix**: e-mail subject to easily filter out incoming e-mails.

. Click the **Test** button on the right to send a test e-mail and verify the setup.

. After successful verification, click **SAVE CHANGES**.
